The image named, 
The_Great_Wave_of_Kanagawa_Maidens_Tower.jpg 
is a combination of two images:

1- The Great Wave off Kanagawa.jpg
Katsushika Hokusai - 1829
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_Wave_off_Kanagawa

2- Maidens Tower.png
The beautiful Maiden's Tower in Bosphorus, Istanbul, Turkey.

In this project;
The drawing style of The Great Wave off Kanagawa was transfered onto Princess Tower,
by using Neural Style Transfer, a special use case of Deep Learning 
by Musa Arda.
